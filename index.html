Mission briefing

Survive escalating infected waves, repair barricades, scavenge supplies, buy weapons from ground caches, and push through increasingly hostile sectors. Every fifth wave contains an elite threat.

Persistent unlocks: higher sectors unlock by completing the previous level. Best wave and high score are stored on this device.

How to play

WASD move · Mouse aim · LMB fire · R reload · F interact/use · 1-3 switch weapons · Esc pause.

Touch controls: left stick moves. Hold and drag the right stick to aim and fire. Use the action buttons for reload, interactions, and weapon switching. Tap Ⅱ to pause.

Kill infected for score. Spend score at weapon caches and barricades. Zombies can drop healing pickups and temporary power-ups. Reloads use unlimited magazines; the HUD only shows rounds currently loaded.
