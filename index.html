Containment Protocol // Top-down Survival

Deployment zone

Mission briefing

Survive escalating infected waves, repair barricades, scavenge supplies, buy weapons from wall crates, and push through increasingly hostile sectors. Every fifth wave contains an elite threat.

Persistent unlocks: higher sectors unlock by surviving previous zones. Best wave and high score are stored on this device.

How to play

WASD move · Mouse aim · LMB fire · R reload · G grenade · F interact/use · 1-3 switch weapons · Esc pause.

Touch controls: left stick moves. Hold and drag the right stick to aim and fire. Use the action buttons for reload, grenades, interactions, and weapon switching. Tap Ⅱ to pause.

Kill infected for score. Spend score at weapon crates and barricades. Enemies drop ammo, armor, grenades, rare healing + pickups, and temporary power-ups. Grenades can clear packed chokepoints. Barricades can be repaired until destroyed.
