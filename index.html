How to play

Survive 10 infected waves, improve your loadout between rounds, and defeat the elite threat every fifth wave.

Touch controls: left stick moves. Hold and drag the right stick to aim and fire.

Zombies can rarely drop healing items, grenades, and temporary add-ons. Buy weapons only between waves. Reloads use unlimited magazines; the HUD shows rounds currently loaded.
